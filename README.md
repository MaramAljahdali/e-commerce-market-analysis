# E-Commerce Market Performance Analysis (2021–2025)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce market performance from 2021 to 2025 using Microsoft Excel and Power BI. The goal is to explore sales, profitability, customer behavior, marketing channel performance, payment methods, and order returns.

The project follows a data analysis workflow, starting with data cleaning and preparation in Excel, followed by data transformation, analysis, and interactive visualization in Power BI.

## Dataset

- **Dataset:** E-Commerce Sales Analytics
- **Source:** [Kaggle – E-Commerce Sales Analytics](https://www.kaggle.com/code/datascikhan/e-commerce-sales-analytics/input)
- **Period:** 2021–2025

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, formulas, PivotTables, and charts.
- **Power Query:** Data transformation and preparation.
- **DAX:** Measures and business calculations.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and data visualization.

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Cleaning & Preparation:** Prepared the dataset in Excel, checked for duplicates and missing values, and applied relevant formulas.
2. **PivotTables & Charts:** Created PivotTables and charts to summarize key business metrics.
3. **Data Transformation:** Used Power Query to prepare the data for analysis in Power BI.
4. **DAX Measures:** Created measures to support business analysis.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Built a dashboard to explore market performance across the five-year period.

## Business Questions & Key Findings

### 1. Sales & Profit Performance

**Question:** How did sales and profits change from 2021 to 2025?

**Visualization:** Sales & Profit (2021–2025)

**Finding:** The dashboard shows relatively stable sales and profit trends across the five-year period, with only small visible changes. This provides an overview of the business's financial performance over time.

### 2. Customer Demographics

**Question:** Which age group contributes the largest share of sales?

**Visualization:** Sales Distribution by Age Group

**Key Findings:**
- **Senior:** 73.6M (41.55%)
- **Adult:** 65.94M (37.23%)
- **Youth:** 37.59M (21.22%)

**Insight:** The Senior age group contributes the largest share of sales, followed by Adults and Youth. This information can help guide customer targeting and marketing strategies.

### 3. Marketing Channel Performance

**Question:** Which marketing channels generated the highest sales from 2021 to 2025?

**Visualization:** Top 5 Marketing Channels Performance

**Key Findings:**
- **Organic Search** shows the highest performance among the five displayed channels throughout the period.
- Google Ads and Direct also contribute substantially.
- Facebook Ads and Instagram show lower values than the other three channels in the displayed chart.

**Insight:** Organic Search is the leading channel in this visualization. The results can help the business evaluate its marketing channel performance and identify areas for further investigation.

### 4. Customer Trends: New vs. Repeat Customers

**Question:** How did new and repeat customer numbers change from 2021 to 2025?

**Visualization:** Customer Retention: New vs Repeat Customers

**Key Findings:**
- Repeat customers make up the largest portion of the displayed customer counts in every year.
- The overall pattern appears relatively stable across the five-year period.
- New customers represent a much smaller portion of the displayed counts.

**Insight:** Repeat customers appear to be an important part of the customer base. However, a retention rate calculation would be needed to measure customer retention more precisely.

### 5. Payment Methods

**Question:** Which payment methods account for the largest shares of sales?

**Visualization:** Sales Distribution by Payment Method

**Key Findings:**
- **Credit Card:** 34.52%
- **Debit Card:** 23.01%
- **PayPal:** 17.28%
- **Digital Wallet:** 13.64%
- **Cash on Delivery:** 11.54%

**Insight:** Credit Card has the largest share of sales, followed by Debit Card and PayPal. Cash on Delivery has the smallest share among the displayed payment methods.

### 6. Order Returns

**Question:** What proportion of orders are returned compared with orders that are not returned?

**Visualization:** Order Return Rate Status

**Key Finding:** The dashboard shows that orders marked as **Not Return** make up the majority of the displayed orders, while returned orders represent a smaller portion.

**Insight:** The return distribution provides an overview of order outcomes. Exact return-rate percentages would help quantify the scale of returns more clearly.

## Dashboard Overview

The Power BI dashboard brings together six areas of analysis:

- Sales and profit trends
- Sales distribution by age group
- Marketing channel performance
- New vs. repeat customer trends
- Sales distribution by payment method
- Order return status

These visualizations provide a consolidated view of business performance and customer behavior from 2021 to 2025.
